Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Overview

ContactLogo ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our applications (iOS, macOS, and Web) handle your personal data and contact information.

On-Device Processing

ContactLogo operates on a strict privacy-first architecture:

  • On-Device Matching: When you grant permission to access your contacts, our engine analyzes company names and organization identifiers directly on your device.
  • No Address Book Uploads: We never upload your full address book, contact phone numbers, personal notes, or private contact details to our servers.
  • Explicit User Approval: No contact records are modified without your explicit review and confirmation.

Data We Collect

We do not collect or store personal identifying information. If you contact customer support, we retain your email address and message history solely to provide assistance.

Third-Party Services

To retrieve brand logos, the app may query public domain asset registries (such as Brandfetch, Clearbit, or official company domains) using only the sanitized business domain name. No personal contact data is included in these lookups.

Your Choices & Data Rights

You can revoke contact permissions at any time via your device's system settings (iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Contacts, or macOS System Settings → Privacy & Security → Contacts).

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our practices, please contact us at support@ContactLogo.com.
